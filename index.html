01 · Tours 101

What is Tours?

TikTok GO Tours lets creators feature real, bookable travel experiences — cruises, guided walks, helicopter rides, skip-the-line tickets and more — directly in their videos. Viewers tap a green product tag to book, and you earn commission on the orders you drive.

04 · Content Guidelines

Two plays to win

Every tour video should be built for one of two goals. Mix and match if you like, but most high-performing content follows one of these plays.

🌅 Play A

Product Seeding

Goal: maximum views + saves. Grow your reach & account.
  • 🎯 Judged on: Scenic hook
  • 🎯 Journey / POV footage
  • 🎯 Storytelling / real experience
🛒 Play B

Hard-Selling

Goal: drive orders / GMV. Make it obvious what to book and how.
  • 🎯 Judged on: Product clarity
  • 🎯 Price / value framing
  • 🎯 Low-friction CTA (+ bundle, social proof)
❗
Whichever play you choose, make sure it highlights at least one of the E-Value Triad.
💰

Economy

Price, promo, deals, discount.

🎓

Education

Itineraries, hacks, hidden gems, authentic reviews, photo ops.

🎉

Entertainment

Beautiful views, must do/try, trends — make it fun.
