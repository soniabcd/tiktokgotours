Tours 101

What counts as TikTok GO Tours?

Tours are bookable local experiences: cruises, sightseeing routes, outdoor adventures, attraction tickets, day trips, guided walks, and more. Your job is to turn a product link into a story viewers want to try.

priority_highMust update TikTok app to participate and for users to view or purchase tours.

Quick product check

Ask: “Is there a named, bookable tour product viewers can tap?” If yes, it belongs in the Tours flow.
